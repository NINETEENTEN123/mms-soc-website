Welcome to the MMS Science Olympiad!

What is the MMS Science Olympiad?

The Virginia Science Olympiad (VASO) is a competition where students from different schools compete in various science-related events. It is a great opportunity for students to showcase their knowledge and skills in science and technology. The 2026-2027 school year will be the first year that Geroge H. Moody Middle School will be participating in the VASO. We are excited to be a part of this competition and look forward to seeing our students excel in the various events. Our middle school is the only middle school in the county that is participating in the VASO, and we are proud to represent our school and community in this prestigious competition. This website serves to help students, parents, and other people connected to this club who would like to participate in our school's chapter. It contains information about the events, links to resources, and contact information for the club advisors. Most of the materials on this website are intended for members who are currently in the club, but we also welcome visitors who are interested in learning more about the VASO and our school's participation in it. For any questions or inquiries, please feel free to reach out to us through the contact page. We look forward to a successful and exciting year in our team!
